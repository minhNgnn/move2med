# gematik brand guide: logo rules (English summary)

> Source: *"Markenleitfaden gematik-Logo"*, Feb 2026, [`docs/source/gematik_Markenleitfaden_Dachmarke.pdf`](../source/gematik_Markenleitfaden_Dachmarke.pdf)
> · Logo files: <https://www.gematik.de/newsroom/mediathek>
>
> The guide is **only about the logo**. It does not define a full UI design system. The colors and fonts below were
> **extracted from the PDF itself** (vector fills and embedded fonts), so treat them as "observed", not official tokens.

## Fonts observed

- **Euclid Circular B** (Bold and Regular) for headings
- **Apercu Pro** (Regular and Bold) for body text

Both are commercial fonts. For a hackathon prototype, close free alternatives from Google Fonts are **Plus Jakarta Sans**
or **DM Sans** for headings and **Inter** for body text.

## Logo rules (pp. 3–10)

- **Standard version:** wordmark plus flag. Other variants only in special cases.
- **Reduced variant** (without flag or tagline), e.g. when the background clashes with the flag colors: **blue** on light
  backgrounds, **white** on dark, **black** only in black-and-white print.
- **Backgrounds:** blue logo on white or light images. White logo on gematik dark blue or dark images. Place it on calm, flat image areas.
- **Clear space:** always horizontal. Clear space on all sides = the **width of the "g"**. Nothing else inside it.
- **Minimum size:** logo **10 mm wide**. With flag, **flag height ≥ 2 mm**.
- **Don'ts:** no distorting, no shadows or effects, no outlines, no color changes, no redrawing. **Use original files only.**
- **Co-branding:** a partner logo may come **after** the gematik logo, separated by a vertical line, and must not look
  bigger. Never in front. **Requires prior written approval.**

## Usage rules (pp. 11–13), which matter for your pitch and demo

- **Editorial / informational use** (newsworthy or public-interest context) is allowed **without permission**, if the
  rules are followed and the logo is unchanged.
- **No implied partnership:** without gematik's consent, don't put the logo next to other company or product logos, and
  **don't use it in a way that suggests gematik is a sender or co-author** (e.g. on your app's login screen or letterhead).
- **No advertising use** without written approval.

> **Practical takeaway:** In the pitch deck, "Challenge by gematik" with their logo on a context slide is editorial use.
> **Don't** put the gematik logo inside your prototype UI as though it were a gematik product. You can borrow the
> *feel* (dark blue `#000E52`, cyan `#009FE3`, lots of white), but give your product its own name and mark.
> If in doubt, ask the gematik mentors at the hackathon.
