## General rules (p. 2)

- A transport may only be prescribed if the trip is **compellingly medically necessary** in connection with a service the
  insurer pays for. The **vehicle** is chosen by medical necessity in the individual case, while staying economical. What
  matters most is the patient's current health and **ability to walk**.
- **Not allowed:** trips without a compelling medical reason (e.g. to arrange appointments, ask for results, pick up
  prescriptions), and trips for long-term-care insurance services (SGB XI), e.g. from home to a nursing home.
- The prescription should be issued **before** the trip.
- Legal basis: **G-BA patient transport guideline (KT-RL)**.
- The prescription is handed to the patient. **No approval needed:** the patient gives it directly to the transport
  company. **Approval needed:** the patient must send it to the **insurer before the trip** for approval.
- **Any change requires the doctor's new signature, stamp and date.**

## Front side: filled by the doctor

### Header (patient block, no number)
Insurer or payer (*Krankenkasse bzw. Kostenträger*) · patient name and date of birth · payer ID (*Kostenträgerkennung*) ·
insurance number (*Versicherten-Nr.*) · status · site number (*Betriebsstätten-Nr.*) · doctor number (*Arzt-Nr.*) · date.

### ❶ Co-payment-free / co-payment required (*Zuzahlungsfrei / Zuzahlungspflicht*)
Patient transport normally **requires a co-payment**, so tick "Zuzahlungspflicht". Tick **"Zuzahlungsfrei"** only:
- when the prescription is charged to an **accident insurance carrier** (see ❷),
- for an **"Versorgungsleiden"** (state-compensated condition, see ❷),
- when the patient **shows proof of exemption**.

### ❷ Accident, consequence of an accident, work accident, occupational disease, Versorgungsleiden (e.g. BVG)
Mark it if applicable. For a **work accident** (including school accidents) or a recognised **occupational disease**, the
prescription is charged to the **accident insurance carrier** (*Unfallversicherungsträger*, e.g. the trade association
*Berufsgenossenschaft*), which must be named in the patient block.
**Versorgungsleiden** = health damage from a publicly ordered or encouraged measure, or caused by a crime, and recognised by
the pension office, e.g. under the **Federal War Victims Act (BVG)**, victims of violent crime, vaccine injuries (Infection
Protection Act), or the Soldiers' Pension Act.

### ❸ Outbound trip / return trip (*Hinfahrt / Rückfahrt*)
Normally only the **direct route** between the patient's location and the **nearest suitable** treatment facility is
covered. The doctor checks medical necessity **separately for the outbound and return trips** (e.g. if only the return
can't be done by public transport, only the return is prescribed). If needed, the transport company's **waiting time** is confirmed in ⓱.
> AOK Rheinland/Hamburg adds: **if outbound and return need different vehicles, each trip must be prescribed separately.**

### 1. Reason for the transport (*Grund der Beförderung*)

**No approval needed (*genehmigungsfreie Fahrten*)**

| # | Box | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ❹ **a)** | Full or partial inpatient hospital treatment, pre- or post-inpatient treatment | No prior approval. For pre-inpatient, give the expected admission date in ⓱. Pre- or post-inpatient trips are limited to **3 treatment days within 14 days** before or after the stay (organ transplant: up to 3 months after). |
| ❺ **b)** | Outpatient treatment for markers **"aG"** (exceptional walking disability), **"Bl"** (blind), **"H"** (helpless), **care level 3 with permanent mobility impairment, care level 4 or 5** — **taxi or rental car only** (a KTW goes under f) | Includes health check-ups and cancer screening. The patient must show the disability card or care-level notice. Care level 3 needs a permanent (≥ 6 months) mobility impairment (marker "G" can be used as evidence). A rental car includes vehicles adapted for disabled people (stretcher, carry chair, own wheelchair): use ⓫ and ⓭. |
| ❻ **c)** | Other reason, e.g. **trips to a hospice** | Also: short-term care facilities, ambulatory surgery that *replaces* a hospital stay (§ 115b SGB V) and its pre- or after-care, medically compelling **transfers** between hospitals, and rescue trips to hospital. Write the reason in the free-text line. Non-replacing ambulatory surgery (e.g. routine cataract) is **not** covered. |

**Approval needed: outpatient treatment (*genehmigungspflichtige Fahrten*, send to the insurer before the trip)**

| # | Box | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ❼ **d)** | **High-frequency treatment: dialysis, oncological chemo- or radiotherapy** | An exception for patients needing these at high frequency (Annex 2 of KT-RL). |
| ❼ | **Comparable exceptional case** | A therapy with high frequency over a long time, where the patient is so affected that transport is essential to avoid harm. Justify in ⓱ (with ICD-10 if possible). |
| ❽ **e)** | **Permanent mobility impairment comparable to b)** and **treatment for at least 6 months** | Justify comparability in ⓱. |
| ❾ **f)** | **Other reason for a trip by KTW**, e.g. **professional positioning, carrying, lifting needed** | When the patient needs **medical attention** or the **special equipment of a KTW** during the trip (e.g. pressure sores), or to avoid transmitting a severe infectious disease. Also used when a patient with marker aG/Bl/H or care level 3–5 needs a **KTW**. Also for non-compelling **transfers** (e.g. to a hospital nearer home); write "Verlegung" (transfer) in ⓱. Justify in ⓬. |

### ❿ 2. Treatment day or frequency and nearest suitable treatment facility
Give the (expected) **treatment date** or **frequency** ("vom/am TTMMJJ / __ x pro Woche, bis voraussichtlich TTMMJJ" =
"from or on DDMMYY / __ times per week, expected until DDMMYY") and the **facility** (name and place). For approval-free trips
the date may be omitted in individual cases (e.g. the appointment comes from an appointment service), with a reason in ⓱.
Insurers only pay up to the **nearest suitable facility**. If the patient chooses a farther one, they pay the difference.

### 3. Type and equipment of transport (*Art und Ausstattung der Beförderung*)

| # | Box | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ⓫ | **Taxi / Mietwagen** (taxi or rental car) | When the patient can't use public transport or a private car for compelling medical reasons. **No medical attention** during the trip. If the patient travels in a wheelchair, carry chair or lying down, tick ⓭ as well. |
| ⓬ | **KTW** (*Krankentransportwagen*, patient transport ambulance), **because medical attention and/or equipment is needed due to …** | Only if a less elaborate vehicle is impossible. **Not the diagnosis but the type and extent of the functional impairment** justifies the KTW (e.g. bleeding risk). State it here (with ICD-10 if possible). ⓭ may also be ticked. |
| ⓭ | **Rollstuhl** (wheelchair) | Patient can't walk and travels **in their own wheelchair** (wheelchair-accessible vehicle). |
| ⓭ | **Tragestuhl** (carry chair) | Patient can't walk, can sit, but **two people must carry them** (e.g. no step-free access). Vehicle needs a carry chair. |
| ⓭ | **liegend** (lying down) | Patient can **only be transported lying down** (vehicle needs a stretcher). |
| ⓮ | **RTW** (*Rettungswagen*, emergency ambulance) | Emergency patients needing measures to maintain or restore vital functions. May be prescribed afterwards in emergencies. |
| ⓯ | **NAW / NEF** (emergency-doctor ambulance or vehicle) | Life-saving measures by an emergency doctor are needed. May be prescribed afterwards. |
| ⓰ | **andere** (other) | E.g. rescue helicopter (RTH), when ground transport isn't fast enough. |

### ⓱ 4. Justification / other (*Begründung / Sonstiges*), free text
Justification of comparability for ❼ and ❽, plus any other relevant info, e.g.:
- expected **admission date** for pre-inpatient trips
- **further planned appointments**, or a frequency that doesn't fit ❿ (e.g. "5× every 2 weeks from … to …")
- reason if the treatment date is unknown, or that the appointment came from an appointment service
- **waiting time** of the transport company between outbound and return trips
- possibility of **shared rides** (*Gemeinschaftsfahrten*) and number of passengers
- **address if the trip doesn't start from or end at the patient's home**
- **weight of heavy patients** (bariatric transport)
- date of the planned ambulatory surgery, or justification for surgery replacing a hospital stay
- that no approval was possible (with time), for unplannable trips
- that a **companion is medically necessary**
- "Verlegung" (transfer)
- the patient needs **intensive ventilation**
- the patient **has a rollator**, or **cannot climb stairs**

Bottom right of the form: **stamp and signature of the doctor**.

## Back side (*Rückseite*): filled by the patient and the transport company

> "The back side is to be filled in by the transport company and the patient." The official back side has three blocks:

### Block 1. *Bestätigung durch den Versicherten* (confirmation by the insured person)
*"Bitte die Fahrt immer durch den Versicherten quittieren lassen!"*: **"Always have the trip signed off by the insured person!"**
*"Ich bestätige die Durchführung der im Folgenden aufgeführten Fahrten"*: "I confirm that the following trips were carried out."

A table with **10 rows**, one per trip:

| Datum (TTMMJJ) | Fahrtstrecke (von … nach) | Hinfahrt ☐ | Rückfahrt ☐ | Unterschrift des Versicherten |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| date | route (from … to) | outbound | return | **patient's signature** |

### Block 2. *Bestätigung des Transporteurs* (confirmation by the transport company)
- "The patient transport was carried out according to the confirmation above."
- "A **valid co-payment exemption card** for the period of the transport was presented: ☐ nein (no) ☐ ja (yes)"
  (not required for rescue trips to hospital)
- **Datum** (date) · **Stempel/Unterschrift des Transporteurs** (company stamp or signature)

### Block 3. *Abrechnungsdaten des Transporteurs* (billing data of the transport company)
| German | English |
|---|---|
| IK des Transporteurs | Company's **Institutionskennzeichen** (9-digit institution ID used for billing insurers) |
| Belegnummer | Receipt or voucher number |
| Gesamt-Brutto | Total gross amount (€) |
| Rechnungsnummer | Invoice number |
| Zuzahlung | Co-payment amount (€) |
| Positionsnummer · Anzahl · km (×2) | Billing position code (tariff item) · quantity · kilometres (two lines) |
